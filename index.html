About us

Search should show its work.

Most search engines hand you a list of links and leave the checking to you. Most AI answers skip the citing altogether. QuentLink sits between the two: an answer engine that verifies its sources before it speaks, and shows them to you afterward, every time.

We're a small team in the early stages of building that engine. More on who we are and how we got here is on its way — for now, the short version is that we got tired of choosing between "fast" and "true," and decided to build something that didn't ask us to.

Accuracy first

Answers are checked against sources before they're shown, not after.

Sources included

Every answer links back to where it came from, in plain view.

Built for trust

If we're not confident, we say so instead of guessing.
